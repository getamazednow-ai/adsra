# Contributing to ADSRA

ADSRA is published as a draft so it can be argued with. This explains how to contribute a
challenge and how your input is handled. It mirrors the ADSRA Peer-Review Contribution Guidelines
(CTR-01 v0.3).

## Where challenge is most wanted — the five open questions

- **OQ-1 Tier boundaries** — is a four-tier classification right, or should Medium split into commercial-sensitive and personal?
- **OQ-2 HYOK guidance** — is hold-your-own-key right as a surgical control, or does it belong more broadly? Bring a real workload.
- **OQ-3 Level 6 governance** — what evidence would convince a regulator that agentic remediation is itself well governed?
- **OQ-4 Utilities and education** — correct or extend the drafted blueprints (Technical Reference §10.5–10.6).
- **OQ-5 Cloud mappings** — correct the indicative AWS / Azure / GCP mappings against current Australian-region capability.

Corrections anywhere else — a wrong date, a broken mapping, a misread instrument — are just as welcome.

## What a strong contribution looks like

- **Concrete.** Cite the instrument, name the workload, bring the counter-example. Anecdote helps; evidence decides.
- **Located.** Name the document and section, slide, pillar or control ID (for example `ADSRA-CR-03`) it touches.
- **Sourced.** Link the primary source and the date you checked it — regulator, legislation, provider documentation.
- **Open about interests.** Say if you work for, or sell, a product the point concerns. It does not disqualify the point.

## How to submit

| Channel | Use it for | Visibility |
|---|---|---|
| **Issues in this repository** — one form per open question, plus a correction form | Challenges, evidence, corrections | **Public** — visible to everyone |
| **The Contribute section of the Assure Govern website** (Control Packs → ADSRA → Contribute) | Anything you would rather not post in public | **Private** — logged for review, not published without your consent |

Either way, include: the open question, pillar or control ID; your point; your evidence; your
sector; and whether you are happy to be credited by name.

## What happens to a submission

1. **Logged.** Recorded as received, with a `PRL-nnn` ID, in the ADSRA Peer-Review Response Log. Nothing is pre-filtered.
2. **Triaged.** Mapped to an open question (`OQ-1`…`OQ-5`) or to a Change Register item (`CR-nnn`), or marked as new.
3. **Evidence check.** Any regulatory claim is checked against its primary source and the evidence register (Technical Reference §12) — never from memory.
4. **Disposition.** Accepted, accepted with modification, rejected, deferred or needs evidence — with the reason recorded. Accepted changes go through the Change Register into the next version.
5. **You are told the outcome** — the disposition, the reason and any `CR-nnn`. Issues are closed with that note.

### Why issues, not pull requests

This repository is a published copy of a privately maintained master: the documents here are
releases, not working files. Accepted contributions are applied to the master and arrive here in
the next release, so please raise them as issues — including corrections to the cloud mappings
(OQ-5). You are credited, and the issue is closed with a note naming the change that carried it.
